# Privacy Policy

Tablica is a browser extension for intelligent tab organization. This privacy policy explains what data Tablica collects, how it is used, and your choices.

## Data Collection

Tablica does **not** collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers. All operations are performed locally in your browser.

## Permissions Explained

- **tabs** — Used to read tab URLs and titles for sorting, grouping, and deduplication. All processing happens locally.
- **tabGroups** — Used to create and manage Chrome tab groups. No data is sent externally.
- **storage** — Used to save your preferences (sort mode, excluded domains, theme) locally in your browser via `chrome.storage.sync`. This data stays within your browser and is only used to restore your settings across devices if you are signed into Chrome.
- **windows** — Used to detect the current window for sort/group/dedup operations.

## Data Storage

Your settings are stored locally in your browser using Chrome's built-in `storage.sync` API. No data is transmitted to external servers.

## Third-Party Services

Tablica does not use any third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising services.

## Updates

This privacy policy may be updated. The latest version will always be available at the extension's documentation page.
